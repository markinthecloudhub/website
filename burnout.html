Burnout Recovery Program

For deep stress, exhaustion and emotional overload

Do you feel constantly tired, tense or overwhelmed? This 3-session program is designed to help your body and mind recover step by step.

Instead of a one-time massage, we work together to:

  • Release built-up tension
  • Calm your nervous system
  • Restore your energy and focus

This is especially supportive for burnout symptoms, chronic stress, expats adjusting to a new environment, and busy professionals.

How the program works

Session one

Body tension release

Massage

We start with a therapeutic Ayurvedic massage focused on:

  • Neck and shoulder tension
  • Back tightness
  • Physical stress stored in the body

This session helps your body switch from 'survival mode' to relaxation.

Result: less muscle pain, deeper breathing, immediate relief
Session two

Nervous system reset

Yoga

In the second session we slow everything down and guide your body into deep rest. This treatment focuses on:

  • Calming the mind
  • Improving sleep quality
  • Reducing anxiety and overstimulation

We use gentle touch and rhythm to support your nervous system.

Result: mental clarity, better sleep, emotional calm
Session three

Energy balance & recovery plan

Reiki

In the final session we work with Reiki to restore inner balance and support emotional recovery. You will also receive:

  • Self-care tips for home
  • Guidance to maintain your energy after the program
Result: feeling grounded, recharged and more in control

For whom is this program?

This program is ideal if you:

  • Feel exhausted even after rest
  • Experience constant stress or pressure
  • Have difficulty sleeping or relaxing
  • Are an expat adjusting to life in the Netherlands
  • Work in a demanding job or care for others
